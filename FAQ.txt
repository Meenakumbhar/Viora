Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) – MemoriesInPrints

1. What services does MemoriesInPrints provide?
We specialize in: 
• Memorial & Funeral Card Designs 
• Tribute Templates 
• Personalized Graphic Design Services 
• Printing & Delivery Solutions (available in select regions) 
• Worldwide digital file delivery

2. Do you provide services worldwide?
✅ Yes. Our digital design services are available globally. 
📦 Printing and physical delivery are available in select countries. 
For international clients, we provide high-resolution digital files that can be printed locally.

3. How do I place an order?
You can place an order directly through our website by selecting a template or requesting a custom design. 
➡️ Once details are confirmed and payment is received, our team will contact you to begin the project and keep you updated throughout the process.

4. How long does it take to receive my order?
• Digital Designs: Typically delivered within 1–3 business days (depending on complexity). 
• Printed Orders: Production usually takes 3–5 business days, plus delivery time depending on your location.

5. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major international payment methods including: 
• Credit/Debit Cards 
• PayPal 
• Bank Transfers (where applicable) 
💡 Note: International clients are responsible for any currency conversion or bank fees.



6. Can I request changes after I receive my design?
Yes. Minor revisions are included in your order. 
✦ Major revisions (significant layout or content changes) outside the agreed scope may require additional charges.

7. Do you offer refunds?
• Digital Services & Custom Designs: Non-refundable once delivered, as they are personalized. 
• Templates: All digital template sales are final. 
• Printing Orders: Can only be canceled before production begins. 
• If an error occurs on our part, we will correct or replace the design at no extra cost.

8. Will my personal information be safe?
Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously. Your information is only used to complete your order and improve our services. 
Please refer to our Privacy Policy for full details.

9. Can I use your designs for commercial purposes?
By default, our designs are for personal use only. 
If you need a commercial license (e.g., resale, business use), please contact us for a tailored agreement.
